# Clone-and-improve vertical SaaS: US hospitality, food & beverage producers, events, outdoor recreation

Research date: 2026-11-07. Scope: US market. 27 candidate niches looked at; 10 shortlisted, 17 killed or folded into other ideas.

**Provenance labels used throughout:**
- **[Capterra cat.]**: Capterra category page fetched 2026-11-07. Each entry gives rating (review count) plus the ease / customer-service / features / value sub-scores.
- **[Capterra rev.]**: Capterra product review page fetched 2026-11-07. Dated quotes come from it.
- **[Latka est.]** and **[Latka rep.]**: GetLatka estimate, or a figure Latka marks as "reported". Treat both as third-party, low precision.
- **[Stripe-verified]**: TrustMRR.
- **[vendor claim]**, **[competitor claim]**, **[aggregator]**: pricing or feature claims from a vendor, from a rival's comparison page, or from third-party software directories.
- **[CBP 2023]**: Census County Business Patterns 2023, US totals (all legal forms). This counts **employer establishments only**, so it leaves out sole-proprietor non-employers and understates micro-businesses such as home bakers, solo DJs and food trucks.

Data note: the Census API now needs a key, so counts come from the CBP 2023 flat file: [cbp23us.zip](https://www2.census.gov/programs-surveys/cbp/datasets/2023/cbp23us.zip). Bing returned off-topic results for long queries (for example, "golf simulator…" returned digestive-system pages), so most discovery went through WebSearch plus direct fetches of Capterra and Latka.

## Q1. Overall: which candidates survive a beatability test (not an absence-of-competition test), and how do they score?

### Takeaway
Ten niches survive. Each has a revenue-proven incumbent whose sub-niche shows one or more of these weaknesses:
- documented dated UX
- roll-up or PE ownership with forced migrations
- per-transaction fees
- quote-only pricing
- no product that dominates the exact workflow

Highest totals:
- Vendor-application/booth software for markets and fairs: 21/25
- Small-caterer / restaurant-catering BEO tool: 19/25
- Small-marina management: 19/25
- Escape-room/experience-venue booking plus gamemaster console: 18/25
- Meal-prep production platform: 18/25
- Agritourism ticketing: 18/25

Several popular ideas are killed because modern, well-rated products already dominate: party rentals, florists, photo booths/DJs, venues, short-term rentals, B&Bs, tour operators, and RV-park metered billing (which is bundled free).

## Q2. Candidate 1: Vendor applications, jurying and booth assignment for craft fairs, makers markets, farmers markets and food-truck rallies (21/25)

### Takeaway
Organizer software for vendor-driven events is proven:
- Eventeny: about $5.9M [Latka est.]
- Zapplication: an incumbent with heavy setup fees
- Marketspread, BoothCentral and Manage My Market: smaller players

Pricing is fee-heavy or setup-heavy, and the art-fair standard (ZAPP) is ill-suited to food and farmers-market vendors. A flat-priced, mobile-first organizer tool with a vendor-side "find events" directory has strong two-sided expansion.

### Cited Findings
**Revenue proof**
- Eventeny: 2025 revenue $5.9M [Latka est.], 54 employees, founded 2018 — [Latka: Eventeny](https://getlatka.com/companies/eventeny).
- Marketspread: 2025 revenue $440K [Latka est.], 4 employees — [Latka: Marketspread](https://getlatka.com/companies/marketspread).
- Manage My Market dates to 2003, and its current website launched around 2008 [vendor history page] — [managemymarket.com](https://www.managemymarket.com).

**Incumbent pricing**
- Zapplication: $1,500 setup plus $2,700/yr software fees, with application fees from $1.99 per application [competitor claim, from Eventeny's comparison page] — [Eventeny vs Zapplication](https://www.eventeny.com/compare/eventeny-vs-zapplication/). ZAPP's own pricing page: [sales.zapplication.org/pricing](https://sales.zapplication.org/pricing).
- Eventeny: $240/mo software fee plus a flat 3% transaction fee on paid plans [vendor's own comparison page] — [Eventeny vs Zapplication](https://www.eventeny.com/compare/eventeny-vs-zapplication/).
- BoothCentral: from $495/event plus $5 per application [competitor claim] — [Eventeny vs BoothCentral](https://eventeny.com/compare/eventeny-vs-boothcentral).
- Marketspread: 2.9% + $0.30 vendor payment processing, plus a $15 per-booth one-time fee for shorter series [vendor] — [Marketspread](https://marketspread.com/manage/farmers-market/).
- Farmles Hub Pro: $99/mo flat [Capterra listing] — [Capterra: Farmles](https://www.capterra.com/p/10041946/Farmles/).

**Beatability evidence**
- ZAPP is described as focused on art and craft shows and "less suited to food vendors, farmers markets or general festival vendors" [third-party roundup] — [TicketFairy: Zapplication alternatives](https://www.ticketfairy.com/compare/event-management/zapplication-alternatives).
- Vendor-side resentment of application fees: r/CraftFairs thread, Apr 2025, "I'm so over application fees" [Reddit, read via a mirror] — [thread](https://reddit.birdcat.cafe/r/CraftFairs/comments/1jynbvy/im_so_over_application_fees/mn23ugm/?context=3).
- The same thread notes that Google Forms is free but lacks jury scoring [Reddit comment] (same link).

### Inferences
**Proposed spin:** flat monthly pricing for organizers running 1–50 events a year, with no percentage take on booth fees beyond Stripe's own fees. The product would cover:
- application forms
- jury scoring
- drag-and-drop booth map (SVG)
- stall-fee collection and waitlists
- document collection for vendors: certificate of insurance (COI), cottage-food permits, health permits, sales-tax IDs
- day-of check-in from a phone

**MVP scope (1–2 months):**
- Multi-event organizer accounts
- Form builder with file uploads
- Jury scoring rubric
- Booth map with assignment
- Stripe Connect payments
- Vendor email and SMS blasts
- Public vendor directory page per event

**Expansion path:**
- A vendor-side "find events near me" marketplace. This creates network effects, since vendors invite organizers.
- Recurring farmers-market seasons with weekly stall billing and SNAP/token reconciliation.
- Food-truck rallies and festivals: the 12,487 mobile food services establishments [CBP 2023] are the vendor-side pool.
- Night markets, flea markets and holiday markets.
- Add-ons: vendor insurance referral revenue, ticketing for attendees, sponsorship packages.

**Acquisition channel used by incumbents:** comparison-page SEO (Eventeny publishes many "vs" pages), state farmers-market associations, and two-sided virality through vendors. All of these are replicable.

**Scores:** Rev 4 · Beat 3 · Copy 5 · Exp 5 · Chan 4 = 21.

### Gaps
- No Capterra or G2 ratings were retrieved for Eventeny, Manage My Market or Zapplication. Capterra's site search returned unrelated products.
- No dated UX complaints about Manage My Market were found. Its beatability rests on its site age and fee structure, not on review evidence.
- The US farmers-market count (USDA AMS directory) and a craft-fair count were not found.

## Q3. Candidate 2: BEO, quote-to-kitchen software for small off-premise caterers and restaurants' catering arms (19/25)

### Takeaway
Catering software is revenue-proven:
- Total Party Planner: 153 Capterra reviews at about $119/mo
- Caterease: claims 30K customers; now owned by Horizon Cloud
- Better Cater: an estimated $330K/yr
- Catering Funnels: an adjacent lead-gen product with Stripe-verified revenue

The legacy leader, Caterease, draws 2025–2026 reviews calling it dated, clunky and "not updated in years". But Total Party Planner (TPP), Better Cater and Flex Catering are well rated, so the wedge has to be narrower:
- restaurants with a catering side-business (258,626 full-service restaurants [CBP 2023])
- small drop-off and corporate caterers that use HoneyBook or spreadsheets today

**Capterra ratings [Capterra cat.]** — [Capterra catering](https://www.capterra.com/catering-software/):

| Product | Rating (reviews) | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| HoneyBook | 4.7 (695) | horizontal |
| Tripleseat | 4.7 (576) | |
| Total Party Planner | 4.8 (153) | |
| STS Cloud | 4.4 (125) | |
| Caterease | 4.4 (111) | value-for-money 4.0 |
| Perfect Venue | 4.8 (82) | |
| Event Temple | 4.9 (76) | |

**Revenue proof**
- Caterease: 2020 revenue $969.5K [Latka rep.], "30K customers", 11 employees, founded 1990, acquired by Horizon Cloud — [Latka: Caterease](https://getlatka.com/companies/caterease). The revenue and customer figures are mutually implausible ($32 per customer per year), so treat both as unreliable.
- Better Cater: 2025 revenue $330K [Latka est.], 3 employees — [Latka: Better Cater](https://getlatka.com/companies/bettercater).
- Tripleseat: 2023 revenue $37.4M [Latka est.] — [Latka: Tripleseat](https://getlatka.com/companies/tripleseat).
- Catering Funnels (done-for-you lead generation for restaurants with catering operations): $4,386 MRR, $30,318 all-time revenue, 8 active subscriptions, founded Nov 2025 [Stripe-verified] — [TrustMRR: Catering Funnels](https://trustmrr.com/startup/catering-funnels).

**Pricing**

- Better Cater: sources conflict, from $470/user/yr to $57–69/mo [aggregator] — [G2 Better Cater](https://g2.com/products/better-cater/pricing).

**Beatability quotes**, all from [Capterra rev.: Caterease](https://www.capterra.com/p/2280/Caterease/reviews/):
- Oct 21, 2026, 3★, "Would Not Purchase Again": "The learning curve was extremely long… ease of use was certainly lacking."
- Mar 26, 2026, 5★: "The software interface looks quite dated and feels clunky compared to modern web applications."
- Nov 27, 2025, 1★, "Driving a Tank on the Freeway": "Hasnt been updated in years… if you want a repair it take weeks to solve."
- Mar 12, 2020, 2★: "you have to log into a citrix client… VERY bare-bones mobile."

Total Party Planner weakness, Apr 9, 2024, 2★: "Subscribed to TPP Pay and they have HIDDEN FEES" — [Capterra rev.: TPP](https://www.capterra.com/p/2278/Total-Party-Planner/reviews/). The newest TPP review parsed on page 1 was from April 2024.

### Inferences
**Proposed spin:** "Catering for restaurants." The workflow:
1. Import the menu from Toast or Square (CSV first).
2. Run the catering inquiry form.
3. Build the quote with per-person pricing and a deposit.
4. E-sign.
5. Auto-generate the BEO, kitchen prep list, pack list and delivery manifest.
6. Collect the balance.

This narrows the field against TPP and Better Cater, which target dedicated caterers. It also complements lead-gen products like Catering Funnels.

**MVP scope (1–2 months):**
- Inquiry form
- Quote builder with menu items, packages, per-person pricing and a fees/tax engine
- Stripe deposits and e-sign
- PDF BEO, prep and pack lists
- Calendar
- Client portal

**Expansion path:**
- Dedicated small caterers (13,222 [CBP 2023])
- Food trucks doing private events (12,487 [CBP 2023])
- Mobile bars
- Meal-prep operators
- Add-ons: embedded payments, an online catering ordering page, staff scheduling, rentals line items, and ezCater-style corporate ordering portals

**Channel:** incumbents rely on Capterra/G2 PPC and directory listings, plus catering trade events. A restaurant-catering spin can also partner with lead-gen agencies like Catering Funnels.

**Scores:** Rev 4 · Beat 3 · Copy 5 · Exp 4 · Chan 3 = 19.

### Gaps
- Which restaurant-catering tools Toast and Square bundle was not verified. If Toast's catering module covers BEOs and prep lists, the restaurant wedge weakens. Check before building.
- No Capterra review-level data was pulled for Better Cater or Flex Catering.

## Q4. Candidate 3: Small-marina / boat-club / dry-stack management (19/25)

### Takeaway
Marina software is proven:
- Storable rolled up Molo and Stellar into "Storable Marine"
- Dockwa: an estimated $5.5M
- DockMaster: since 1984, about $2.5M estimated

Reviews show:
- DockMaster's UI called "extremely outdated" (Sep 2025)
- complaints about Storable/Molo's payment-processing hold and costs (2023–2025)
- Dockwa's modular pricing at about $200 per feature per month

That leaves room for an affordable, all-in-one tool for the long tail of small marinas, boat clubs and dry-stack operators.

### Cited Findings
**Revenue proof**
- Storable acquired Molo in Aug 2021 — [AlleyWatch](https://alleywatch.com/2021/08/molo-acquired-storable-marina-management-storage/).
- Storable acquired Stellar (boat rentals) in Oct 2022 — [Storable](https://www.storable.com/about-us/news/stellar-joins-molo/).
- The products were rebranded Storable Molo and Storable Rentals — [finanzen.net](https://www.finanzen.net/nachricht/aktien/storable-unifies-marine-software-solutions-under-new-storable-marine-brand-15304523).
- Molo's 2021 revenue was $780.7K [Latka rep., pre-acquisition] — [Latka: Molo](https://getlatka.com/companies/molo).
- Dockwa: 2025 revenue $5.5M [Growjo estimate via Latka], 47 employees — [Latka: Dockwa](https://getlatka.com/companies/dockwa).
- A Dockwa deal to acquire Marinas.com was also listed — [MarketScreener](https://ca.marketscreener.com/news/latest/Dockwa-Inc-to-Acquire-Marinas-com-23744417).
- DockMaster: 2025 revenue $2.5M [Latka est.], founded 1984 — [Latka: DockMaster](https://getlatka.com/companies/dockmaster).

**Ratings and pricing**
- Storable Marine (Molo): 4.3 (57) [Capterra rev. aggregate] — [Capterra: Molo](https://www.capterra.com/p/141098/Molo/reviews/).
- DockMaster: 3.9 (18) [Capterra rev. aggregate] — [Capterra: DockMaster](https://www.capterra.com/p/92549/DockMaster/reviews/).
- Dockwa: 4.9 (16), but the reviews are mostly from 2020–21. G2 shows 4.3 from 7 reviews. Starting price is $200 per feature per month with no free trial [aggregator] — [Capterra: Dockwa pricing](https://www.capterra.com/p/147452/Dockwa/pricing/); [G2: Dockwa](https://www.g2.com/products/dockwa-dockwa).
- Dockwa's own page says pricing is quote-based by module, with a free marina profile — [Dockwa pricing](https://marinas.dockwa.com/marina-software-pricing).

**Beatability quotes**
- DockMaster, Sep 23, 2025, 1★: "The user interface is extremely outdated… Always behind when it comes to features." — [Capterra: DockMaster](https://www.capterra.com/p/92549/DockMaster/reviews/)
- DockMaster, Oct 27, 2025, 4★: "clunky not intuitive. Bugs are constantly being found." — same page
- Molo, Feb 12, 2025, 2★: "Customer pays, Molo collects, you will never have a definitive deposit from Molo to you. They keep your money!" — [Capterra: Molo](https://www.capterra.com/p/141098/Molo/reviews/)
- Molo, Jul 31, 2023, 3★: "Costs are outrageous." — same page
- Molo, Aug 2, 2023, 2★: charged from the start of onboarding despite promises otherwise. — same page

**Market size and add-ons**
- 3,739 marina establishments [CBP 2023].
- Utility-meter integration vendors already serve marinas. Wild Energy integrates with Park software for campgrounds and marinas — [Wild Energy](https://wildenergyco.com/blogs/press-releases/park-software-and-wild-energy-announce-utility-billing-integration-for-campgrounds-and-marinas).

### Inferences
**Proposed spin:** one flat price, not per-module, for marinas with fewer than 150 slips, boat clubs, municipal marinas and dry-stack operators. The product would cover:
- interactive slip and rack map
- annual and seasonal contracts with e-sign
- autopay with payouts going directly to the marina's own Stripe account (an answer to the Molo payout complaint)
- transient reservations widget
- launch-request queue for dry-stack (customers request a launch by text or app)
- work orders
- waitlist

**MVP scope (2–3 months):** slip/rack inventory map, customer and vessel records, contracts and recurring billing, transient booking page, dry-stack launch queue (a mobile PWA for boaters), simple fuel-dock sales log.
- No channel certifications needed.
- Fuel-pump and meter hardware integrations come later; Wild Energy offers an API partnership.

**Expansion path:**
- boat clubs (membership plus reservations)
- yacht clubs
- mooring fields
- boat, RV and trailer storage lots (Storable's core self-storage adjacency)
- boat rentals (Stellar's niche)
- Add-ons: embedded payments, metered utilities, service and parts work orders, insurance-document tracking

**Channel:** marina trade associations and conferences, plus migration offers aimed at Molo/Storable and DockMaster users. Dockwa's boater-side app is a distribution moat that is hard to copy, so this lowers Chan to 3.

**Scores:** Rev 4 · Beat 4 · Copy 4 · Exp 4 · Chan 3 = 19.

### Gaps
- The US marina count beyond CBP employer establishments (association counts) was not found.
- Dockwa's current ownership or funding beyond the Latka/Growjo estimate was not verified.
- Whether Storable Marine has cut prices for small marinas was not checked.

## Q5. Candidate 4: Escape rooms and small experience venues (axe throwing, rage rooms, VR) — booking, waivers and gamemaster console in one (18/25)

### Takeaway
Escape rooms are a stable niche of about 2,000 US facilities and 7,800 rooms. Their booking software is proven:
- Bookeo: 103 Capterra reviews; claims 40K customers
- Resova: claims 1,300+ venues
- Xola: an estimated $4.5M
- FareHarbor

But it is dated, has weak support, or is fee-based. Gamemaster/hint software is a separate purchase. Bundling booking, waivers and a gamemaster console solves an exact-workflow gap. The adjacent full-FEC leader, ROLLER, is drawing 2025–26 "overpriced" and "support fell off a cliff" reviews.

- The US had "just over 2,000" escape-room facilities and about 7,800 rooms in Dec 2025, flat versus 2023–24 — [Room Escape Artist, Dec 2025](https://roomescapeartist.com/2025/12/29/us-escape-room-industry-report-december-2025/).

### Inferences
- The kill list shows "competition exists" is not the issue. The issue is competitors that are rated about 4.8–5.0 with 2025–2026 reviews and low price points: Check Cherry, Goodshuffle, Details Flowers, OwnerRez. Those niches would need a sub-sub-niche plus a distribution edge that this research did not find.
- RV-park metered billing is a clean example of the "core software bundles it free" kill criterion.

### Gaps
- Mobile-bar/bartending businesses and food-truck commissary scheduling were searched, but the results were off-topic, so they remain unassessed.
- Ghost-kitchen incumbents (e.g., Otter, Deliverect) were not verified this round. The kill rests on the integration-dependency inference.
- Golf-course tee-sheet software was out of scope: golf courses run on POS-heavy suites, and Lightspeed Golf (4.7 (176)) and GolfStatus (4.9 (361)) are well rated — [Capterra golf](https://www.capterra.com/golf-course-software/).
