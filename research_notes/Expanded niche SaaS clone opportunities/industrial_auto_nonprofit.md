# Clone-and-improve vertical SaaS: construction subs, logistics, automotive, industrial services, death care, nonprofit and local government (US, as of 2026-11-07)

Scope note: 27 candidates evaluated. 9 shortlisted with evidence and scores. 18 killed, with reasons. Candidates were judged on whether the incumbents can be beaten, not on whether competition exists. Every figure is labelled by where it came from:
- **[Capterra cat.]**: Capterra category listing, last updated Oct 21 to Nov 6, 2026 (fetched 2026-11-07).
- **[Capterra prod.]**: Capterra product or review page (fetched 2026-11-07).
- **[vendor]**: the vendor's own pricing page or claim.
- **[Latka est.]**: Latka estimate. Low reliability; see the last section.
- **[third-party]**: comparison blog or aggregator.
- **[press]**: press release or trade news.

The scores (1–5 on each of five axes, /25) are my own judgments built from the cited evidence. They are not sourced facts.

## Q0. Which candidates made the shortlist, and how do they rank?

### Takeaway
The strongest opportunities fall into three patterns:
1. **Compliance-reporting field apps for small trade firms**, where PE-backed incumbents aim upmarket: small-shop fire-protection inspection, a backflow tester app, small-sub T&M/change-order/pay-app tools.
2. **Legacy desktop or roll-up-neglected systems in sticky niches**: Catholic parish sacramental ChMS, BHPH used-car DMS, small-town permitting.
3. **Ops software where a single PE-backed leader sells demo-only on annual contracts**: liquid waste (septic and portable sanitation), and welding/fab job shops.

## Candidate 1. Small fire-protection inspection app (extinguishers, emergency lighting, kitchen hood, then sprinkler/alarm/backflow)

### Takeaway
There is clear revenue proof (two PE growth investments in 2025, plus ServiceTrade). The main US incumbent built for this niche rates poorly, and funded players are aiming at mid-market and large fire-protection firms. A cheap, offline, NFPA-10-first app for 1–10-tech extinguisher and service shops is beatable. It expands naturally into NFPA 25/72/96, backflow and jurisdiction report submission. **Score 20/25.**

### Cited Findings
**Original and revenue proof**

- Inspect Point acquired FormLink Systems in Aug 2023 and FireCAD on Apr 16, 2025 [press]. — [SecurityInfoWatch](https://www.securityinfowatch.com/home/press-release/53068323/inspect-point-acquires-formlink-systems); [Journal of Cyber Policy](https://journalofcyberpolicy.com/?p=310255); [Field Technologies Online, Nov 12 2025](https://www.fieldtechnologiesonline.com/doc/inspect-point-announces-integration-with-firecad-unifying-fire-life-safety-design-inspection-0001)

Uptick later opened a Denver hub to expand in North America. It claims 1.6M buildings and 26M pieces of equipment are maintained on its software each year [vendor]. — [PSG](https://www.psgequity.com/news/uptick-announces-strategic-growth-investment-from-psg); [ChannelLife US](https://channellife.news/story/uptick-opens-denver-office-to-boost-north-america-push); [Intl Fire & Safety Journal](https://internationalfireandsafetyjournal.com/psg-backs-uptick-to-expand-global-fire-protection-software/)

**Incumbents in the sub-niche**

**Beatability evidence: dated Inspect Point review quotes [Capterra prod.]** — [Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/148287/Inspect-Point/reviews/)

**Integration and compliance hook**

### Inferences
- **Spin:** "Extinguisher route app" for 1–10-tech shops. Features:
  - barcode or QR tag per extinguisher
  - NFPA 10 monthly/annual/6-yr/12-yr hydro scheduling
  - offline mode
  - deficiency photo to quote to invoice
  - customer portal with certificates
  - flat price (e.g. $79–149/mo per company, not per user)
  - one-click export or upload to TCE-style portals
- **MVP (6–10 weeks):** customers, sites, assets, recurring inspections, mobile checklist with photos, PDF report, Stripe invoicing, QuickBooks Online sync.
- **Expansion:**
  - add NFPA 96 hood suppression, NFPA 101 emergency lighting, NFPA 25 sprinkler, NFPA 72 alarm and backflow forms (shares Candidate 3's form engine)
  - embedded payments
  - parts ordering from distributors
  - a "deficiency to proposal" upsell engine
  - property-manager multi-site portals
- **Channel:** incumbents rely on trade shows (NFPA, NAFED), acquisitions of form vendors (FormLink) and partnerships. A solo founder can use state licensee lists of extinguisher technicians (many state fire marshals publish them), NAFED chapters, YouTube demos, and Facebook trade groups. Channel score is 3, because the leads are reachable but the trade is conservative.
- **Risk:** Uptick plus a Denver hub plus PSG money could move down-market. ZenFire is already well rated.

## Candidate 2. Small trade-sub T&M tags + change-order (COR) log + AIA G702/G703 pay app + lien waivers

### Takeaway
There is strong VC-funded revenue proof (Clearstory, Siteline) and per-user incumbents (Raken). Clearstory's free tier (5 T&M tags/month) is a real threat. The opening is a flat-priced bundle for 5–50-person specialty subs that ties field T&M signatures to the COR log and the monthly pay app. Today those are three separate tools, and the paid ones are quote-only. **Score 20/25.**

### Cited Findings
**Revenue proof**
- Clearstory (formerly Extracker) raised a $16M Series B led by Prudence in Jun 2024, bringing the total to $35M. It says it handles more than 19,000 change orders worth over $850M every month [press, vendor-claimed]. — [Pulse 2.0](https://pulse2.com/clearstory-change-order-construction-communication-platform-company-raises-16-million-in-series-b); [Clearstory press room](https://www.clearstory.build/press-room)
- Siteline raised a $15M Series A led by Menlo Ventures (Feb 2022), for $18.4M in total [press]. It claims subcontractors have billed over $14B across 250k+ projects on Siteline [vendor]. Latka estimates 2025 revenue at $4.7M [Latka est.]. — [yespress/Siteline](https://yespress.io/siteline); [Siteline pricing](https://www.siteline.com/pricing); [Latka](https://getlatka.com/companies/siteline)

**Incumbents and pricing**
- Clearstory has three tiers [vendor]. — [Clearstory pricing](https://www.clearstory.build/pricing)
  - Basic: "Free for all stakeholders", with T&M tags and CORs capped at 5/month.
  - Standard and Professional: "Contact Sales", priced per user plus a platform fee per project.
- Siteline pricing is "custom… To receive a quote" [vendor]. — [Siteline pricing](https://www.siteline.com/pricing)
- Raken (daily reports and time) costs $23/user/mo (Basic) to $59/user/mo (Performance) [vendor]. — [Raken pricing](https://www.rakenapp.com/pricing)
- Fieldwire is rated 4.6 (99) [Capterra cat.]. — [Capterra punch list](https://www.capterra.com/punch-list-software/)
- Procore is rated 4.5 (2,680) [Capterra cat.]. — [Capterra subcontractor](https://www.capterra.com/subcontractor-software/)

### Inferences
- **Beatability:** this is beatable on price and packaging, not on UX. Clearstory's model leans on GCs inviting subs, and paid tiers are priced per user and per project. A small electrical or drywall sub doing 10–40 T&M tickets a month exceeds the free cap, yet resists a sales-led contract.
- **Spin:** a flat $99–199/mo "T&M-to-cash" tool for subs. Features:
  - field ticket with crew hours, materials and GC super's e-signature (works offline)
  - auto-priced from the sub's labor and material rates
  - rolls into a COR log
  - approved CORs flow into the next G703 schedule-of-values line
  - conditional and unconditional lien waivers attached
- **MVP:** e-sign T&M ticket, rate tables, COR log, PDF G702/G703 generator, waiver templates, email delivery.
- **Expansion:**
  - retainage tracking
  - certified payroll (WH-347) for prevailing-wage jobs
  - daily logs
  - embedded receivables financing or early-pay partnerships
- **Channel:** incumbents use GC-mandate virality (a GC requires subs on the platform) plus enterprise sales. A solo founder can use trade-association chapters (NECA, ABC, ASA), YouTube and LinkedIn foremen content, and templates SEO (for example "T&M ticket template" or "AIA G703 Excel").

### Gaps
- No sourced count of US specialty-trade contractor establishments. The Census API needs a key, so the NAICS 238 count is missing.
- Clearstory's paid list prices are not public.

## Candidate 3. Backflow tester app (offline-first) that submits into purveyor systems

### Takeaway
The leading tester-side product, Syncta, is owned by a manufacturer (Watts, since 2018). Its public reviews are few and mostly from 2021–2023. Reviewers complain about no offline mode, rigid due-date logic and clunky workflows. Purveyor-side systems (BSI Online, 550+ purveyors) charge or control submission. An offline-first tester app that fills each jurisdiction's form and exports to BSI or Tokay-style formats is a credible wedge. Revenue proof is moderate. **Score 19/25.**

### Cited Findings
**Original and revenue proof**
- Watts acquired Syncta's technology in April 2018. Syncta offers "Pay as you go" and "Per Tester" plans; amounts not shown [vendor]. — [Syncta our story](https://syncta.com/our-story); [Syncta testers](https://www.syncta.com/backflow-testers)
- BSI Online says it has partnered with over 550 water purveyors (per a city page) [vendor via municipality]. Cities sign subscription agreements with BSI (e.g. Madera, CA, 2021). — [Madera BSI agreement](https://madera.gov/wp-content/uploads/2021/01/D-1-01.20.21-BSI-Online-Subscription-Agreement.pdf); [Round Rock tester instructions](https://www.roundrocktexas.gov/wp-content/uploads/2022/09/BSI-Online-Tester-Instructions-USA_9.6.22-3.pdf)

**Incumbent rating**
- Syncta: 4.3★ from 14 reviews [Capterra prod.]. — [Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/208106/Syncta/reviews/)

**Beatability: dated Syncta review quotes [Capterra prod.]** — [Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/208106/Syncta/reviews/)
- Nov 8, 2021: "If you do not have cell service, you have no access to database in any way… It is crazy that there is [n]o offline aspect."
- Nov 8, 2021: "The mobile app is not great… If you don't have perfect service, you get an error and have to restart the app."
- Jan 23, 2023 (1.0): "Syncta can only calculate next test due date one way or the other… you will find yourself maintaining test due dates manually."
- Nov 8, 2021: "Clunky in places… you have to first review, then jump to the drop down menu and go to submit…"

**TAM**
- EPA: small systems serving 10,000 or fewer people are more than 92% of the nation's ~51,000 community water systems [gov]. — [EPA small system variances](https://www.epa.gov/dwregdev/small-drinking-water-system-variances)
- Testers charge customers roughly $55–90 per device test (two tester sites) [third-party]. — [Syncta-hosted tester pages, e.g.](https://apex-backflow-services.syncta.com)

### Inferences
- **Spin:** a tester-first app. Features:
  - offline test entry
  - jurisdiction form library (state- and purveyor-specific report layouts)
  - flexible due-date rules per purveyor
  - automatic customer reminders
  - Stripe invoicing
  - one-tap PDF and email submission, or export into BSI Online, Tokay and similar purveyor systems
  - priced flat per tester (e.g. $29–49/mo)
- **MVP (4–8 weeks):** customers, devices, test-kit calibration records, test form with pass/fail math, PDF output, reminders, payments.
- **Expansion:**
  - add fire-sprinkler backflow forms (overlaps Candidate 1)
  - irrigation-contractor workflows
  - repair parts quoting
  - a lightweight purveyor dashboard for very small water systems that can't afford BSI or Tokay
  - annual "auto-test" subscription billing for testers' customers (Syncta tester sites already advertise auto-annual testing)
- **Channel:** purveyors publish approved-tester lists, which are scrapeable. Other channels: ABPA and ASSE chapters, certification courses, and tester Facebook groups. Channel score is 4.
- **Risk:** where a purveyor mandates its own portal, the app's value drops to field capture plus billing.

### Gaps
- No US count of certified backflow testers found.
- No Syncta, BSI or Tokay revenue figures found.
- Tokay, XC2 and VEPO were not verified in this session.

## Candidate 6. Catholic parish sacramental-register ChMS

### Takeaway
The two legacy Catholic-specific systems show clear beatability signals.
