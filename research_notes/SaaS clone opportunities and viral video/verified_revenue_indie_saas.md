### Inferences
- The strongest "copy the business model, not the product" signal is Tree Nerd Academy:
  - one SME-founder, one credential, a Next.js + Stripe build, $199 one-time pricing with a pass guarantee

**Original and traction**

- Fire-alarm installers and inspectors who need NICET certification.

## Q3. Which acquisition channel did the original use (SEO, marketplace listing, Product Hunt, ads), and could a newcomer replicate it?

### Takeaway
- The durable verified winners in this sweep grew through content/SEO (Glossa), undisclosed low-SEO channels (Tree Nerd), or community-first launches (Packager: free Reddit launch, then Microsoft MVP partner demos and YouTube).
- These are replicable by a newcomer without an audience.
- The originals that grew through Meta ads or influencers (Marky, Journable, MonAI, Locked) fail the "demand isn't bought" criterion.

- Marketing channels: "content marketing and SEO." Domain rating 48, 107 X followers. — [TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/glossa-live.md)

### Inferences
- **NICET prep (Shortlist 1):**
  - Tree Nerd's high traffic against a low domain rating implies non-search channels: likely YouTube, trade Facebook groups or industry associations. This is an inference; the channel is undisclosed.
  - A newcomer could replicate it through fire-alarm trade communities and employer team licences.
  - The founder (or a co-founder) needs to be or hire a credible NICET-certified instructor.
- **Mac Intune packager (Shortlist 2):** Packager's playbook transfers directly: a free launch in r/Intune and Mac-admin communities, then demos by Microsoft MVP or Mac-admin influencers. This is the most replicable channel in the set (inference).
- **K-12/municipal translation (Shortlist 3):** Glossa's SEO channel is replicable but slow, given its domain rating of 48. B2G buyers often buy through procurement or RFPs rather than self-serve search. That weakens replicability for a solo founder (inference).
- **Channels to avoid copying:** Marky-style Meta-ads growth needs a paid budget, and the influencer/UGC apps depend on bought or borrowed reach. Both fail screening criterion 2.

### Gaps
- Tree Nerd Academy, Hirevire, FreeTCF and SoldComps do not disclose acquisition channels on TrustMRR.
- Product Hunt launch data was not checked for any candidate.
- No Packager traffic or SEO data was available; the URL was not found.
