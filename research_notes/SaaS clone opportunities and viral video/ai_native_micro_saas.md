# AI-native micro-SaaS to clone and niche down (US, as of 6 Nov 2026): proven revenue vs commoditisation risk

**Scope and method.** Research date: 2026-11-06. AI video creation and editing tools are excluded. Already-assessed ideas (GLP-1 tracker, Excel formula bots, advisor LinkedIn writers, Claude/ChatGPT organisers, app builders, ad-library scrapers) were not revisited.

**Provenance labels used on every figure:**
- **[TrustMRR-verified]**: revenue pulled by TrustMRR from the founder's Stripe or other payment-provider API key. Each is a snapshot on the date shown. It is the best available, but not audited.
- **[founder-claimed]**: interview or post by the founder.
- **[vendor page, fetched 2026-11-06]**: live vendor page read today.
- **[vendor-claimed]**: the vendor's own marketing.
- **[secondary]**: blog, listicle or competitor comparison. These often conflict.
- **[third-party estimate]**: app-intelligence or analyst estimate.

**Research limits.**
- The web-search budget ran out partway through, after roughly 60 searches and fetches. Two planned niche checks (independent insurance adjusters, residential appraisers) were not run. Three candidates flagged "pending" in Q4 never got their mandatory niche-incumbent check.
- I did not fetch Reddit, Sensor Tower or Appfigures paid data.
- Many 2026 sources are SEO or vendor blogs. They are labelled as such.

## Q1. Which AI micro-SaaS reached durable revenue quickly in 2025–2026, and what channel drove it?

### Takeaway
Fast, durable AI micro-SaaS revenue is rare.
- TrustMRR's AI cohort is large but mostly tiny. One aggregator reports a $1,539 average MRR across 1,441 AI startups and a $177 median among those under $5k.
- The 2025–26 products verified at **$2.8k–$52k MRR within 5–14 months** share a pattern. Each has a narrow professional user (screenwriters, Blender artists, patent filers, law-firm voicemail) and does a job that general chatbots don't do natively.
- The best-documented durable *single-job* tool is still Bank Statement Converter. It grew slowly (2021 to about $40k MRR in mid-2025, founder-claimed) on organic search and word of mouth after paid ads failed.

- The chatbot and agency-resale products in Customer Support show the **agency/white-label channel** (ChatDash $601k total, anon-2 $445k total) is real. But those businesses sell to resellers, not end users.

## Q2. Which AI categories have collapsed in price or been absorbed by foundation-model features or the vertical's core software (2026)?

### Takeaway
Of the 15 categories assigned, 12 are commoditised or saturated in 2026 and should not be the base for a clone. Three mechanisms drive this:
1. **Free model-native features**: Gemini Nano Banana headshots and staging, ChatGPT Study Mode, YouTube auto-dubbing, ChatGPT Record.
2. **Vertical core software bundling the AI as a cheap add-on**:
   - Jobber Receptionist (reported $29/mo add-on)
   - SimplePractice Note Taker (~$35), Jane AI Scribe (~$15), TherapyFuel (~$40)
   - Spectora AI Report Assist
   - QuickBooks Accounting Agent
   - PAR AI Report Writer
3. **Per-unit price collapse**: AI staging at $1–3 a photo vs $20–50 human; Fin at $0.99 per resolution; OCR at $1–4 per 1,000 pages.

On top of this, Google AI Overviews cut organic clicks by roughly 40%. That hurts both AI-SEO content tools and SEO-dependent clones.

## Q3. Voice/receptionist agents and document extraction: what do per-minute and per-page costs look like, and which niches remain unserved?

### Takeaway
**Voice:**
- All-in API cost is about **$0.07–$0.31 per minute**. Retell's live default example is **$0.11/min**: LLM $0.04 + voice infrastructure $0.055 + TTS $0.015, with telephony at $0.015/min when you use Retell numbers.
- A 3-minute call therefore costs about **$0.33–0.45**. At Jobber's reported $0.79 overage per conversation, or the ~$0.20/min SMB plans (Rosie), gross margin is only about **40–60%** before support.
- **Every niche I checked was already served.** The trades are bundled by Jobber, Housecall Pro and ServiceTitan. Law firms are covered by Frontdesk, Dialzara, Nuacom and others. Horizontal vendors publish programmatic per-vertical landing pages, so "AI receptionist for [X]" nearly always already exists.
- New solo entrants on TrustMRR mostly sit at $0–$2k MRR.

**Document extraction:**
- Model cost is about **$0.0002–$0.004 per page**: Mistral OCR $1–4 per 1,000 pages; Gemini Flash-class roughly $0.2–2 per 1,000 pages.
- Retail is **$0.18–0.48 per page** (DocuClipper live), so gross margin on model cost is above 95%.
- But **every vertical document niche I checked already has dedicated tools**: loss runs, trucking rate confirmations, family-law bank analysis, text-message exhibits, auction lots, HOA violation letters.

### Cited Findings

**Voice-agent unit costs**

### Inferences
- **Voice is a bad first clone for a solo founder in 2026.** Variable cost is $0.07–0.31/min, and the LLM is the swing factor. Incumbents anchor prices at $29/mo, $0.79 per conversation or $0.99 per resolution. Bundling and programmatic vertical pages cover the niches. A solo build would need either (a) a vertical whose system of record has no telephony partner, or (b) a non-receptionist voice job such as voicemail triage, outbound confirmations or after-hours dispatch. I did not find an unserved US example of either.
- **Document extraction has excellent unit economics:** a 10-page statement costs under $0.04 in model spend vs $1.80–4.80 retail. But the market has already been niched down. The open space is "1% better generic" (reliability, coverage of any bank or credit union, balance checks, price) sold through long-tail search, not a new vertical.
